I completed my master's thesis at the **University of Pisa** in 2026 under the supervision of **Professor Marco Romito**.

**Title:** *Flow Equation Approach for the Stochastic Navier-Stokes Equations in the Full Subcritical Regime*.

The thesis concerns the flow equation approach to the stochastic Navier–Stokes equations in the full subcritical regime.

### Related work

My earlier [presentation on the flow equation approach to a singular SPDE](/projects/flow-equation-spde), also supervised by Professor Marco Romito, introduces the approach in the setting of a singular elliptic equation. That project page includes the presentation slides and references.
